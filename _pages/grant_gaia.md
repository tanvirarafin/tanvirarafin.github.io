### Project Summary

AI data centers rely heavily on GPU clusters for training and inference of large models. This project investigates **microarchitecture-level power side-channel attacks** targeting GPU hardware in AI data center environments, and develops corresponding countermeasures.

The GAIA project aims to:

- Characterize power consumption patterns exposed by GPU microarchitectural features during ML workloads
- Develop practical side-channel attack methodologies that can extract sensitive model weights or intermediate data
- Design and evaluate hardware and software countermeasures to mitigate these vulnerabilities
