### Project Summary

Critical energy infrastructure — power grids, substations, and control systems — is increasingly exposed to cyber threats that exploit the boundary between analog sensing and digital control. CASPER develops **analog-cyber sensing techniques** to detect and mitigate attacks targeting these interfaces.

The CASPER project aims to:

- Identify vulnerabilities at the analog-digital boundary in energy control systems
- Develop hardware-based sensing primitives that can detect manipulation of analog measurements and control signals
- Build lightweight anomaly-detection mechanisms suitable for resource-constrained embedded systems in energy infrastructure
