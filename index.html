Research thrusts

01 — 03
DAC · DATE · PACT · ICCD

Accelerators for post-quantum & verifiable computing

NTT, sum-check, and hash-chain kernels mapped onto Versal AI Engines and edge GPUs so zkSNARKs, FHE, and XMSS run within an embedded power budget.

HOST · AsianHOST · GOMACTech

Roots of trust & side channels

Power, electromagnetic, and timing leakage in NTT and lattice hardware. Fault injection against hash-based post-quantum signatures, and the countermeasures that survive it.

HOST · MOST · NSF CyberTraining

Secure autonomy & transportation

LiDAR spoofing against SLAM, ROS 2 attack surfaces, CAN-bus exploitation, and the cyberinfrastructure that trains the next automotive security workforce.
